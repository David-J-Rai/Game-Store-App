Our Mission

We are dedicated to helping gamers find their next favourite adventure without breaking the bank.

The MyGameStore Story

Founded in 2026, MyGameStore started with a simple idea: gaming should be accessible, affordable, and fun for everyone. As passionate gamers ourselves, we know how tough it can be to keep up with the latest releases and hidden indie gems. That's why we built a curated space to bring you great games at great prices.

What We Stand For

  • Community First: We build our catalog around what we believe players actually want to play.
  • Fair Pricing: We believe epic stories and competitive thrill shouldn't cost a fortune.
  • Pure Passion: Every title featured on our store is handpicked by people who love games.
